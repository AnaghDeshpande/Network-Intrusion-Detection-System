Key Takeaways

    Accuracy alone is misleading for imbalanced datasets.

    Before SMOTE, accuracy was slightly higher because the model correctly predicts majority classes but ignores rare attacks.

    SMOTE improves minority class detection.

    This is critical in intrusion detection: catching rare attacks (r2l, u2r) is more important than slightly higher overall accuracy.

    Trade-off:

    Slight drop in overall accuracy (or precision in majority classes)

    Significant improvement in detecting minority classes → better real-world performance for NIDS.

    SMOTE-applied model is better for NIDS, even if overall accuracy is slightly lower, because it detects rare attacks more reliably.
